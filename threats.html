Major Threats

🎣 Phishing

Fraudulent emails, messages, and websites designed to steal personal information.

  • Check sender details
  • Avoid suspicious links
  • Never share OTPs

🦠 Malware

Viruses, worms, trojans, and spyware that infect devices and steal data.

  • Use antivirus software
  • Update your system
  • Download from trusted sources

🔒 Ransomware

Encrypts files and demands payment to restore access.

  • Create backups
  • Update software
  • Avoid unknown attachments

👤 Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate victims into revealing confidential information.

  • Verify requests
  • Stay alert
  • Protect personal data

🔑 Password Attacks

Hackers use stolen or weak passwords to access accounts.

  • Use strong passwords
  • Enable 2FA
  • Don't reuse passwords

🌐 DDoS Attacks

Overloads websites and servers with massive traffic.

  • Use firewalls
  • Monitor traffic
  • Use DDoS protection

Cyber Timeline

2010

Stuxnet Worm

A sophisticated malware that targeted industrial control systems and became one of the most famous cyber weapons.

2017

WannaCry Ransomware

A global ransomware attack that infected hundreds of thousands of computers across more than 150 countries.

2018

Facebook Data Breach

Millions of users were affected when personal data was improperly accessed and exposed.

2020

Twitter Bitcoin Hack

Several high-profile accounts were compromised and used to promote cryptocurrency scams.

2021

Colonial Pipeline Attack

A ransomware attack disrupted fuel distribution and highlighted risks to critical infrastructure.

2024

CDK Global Ransomware Incident

A major ransomware attack impacted automotive dealerships and business operations.

Why Threat Awareness Matters

Understanding cyber threats helps users recognize risks, avoid scams, and protect sensitive information from attackers.
