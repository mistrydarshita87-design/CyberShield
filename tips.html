Cybershield Safety Tips

Learn simple yet effective cybersecurity practices to stay safe online, protect your accounts, and reduce cyber risks.

Quick Security Tips

Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords with letters, numbers, and symbols.

Enable 2FA

Add an extra layer of protection using Two-Factor Authentication.

Beware of Phishing

Check sender details before clicking links or downloading files.

Safe Browsing

Visit trusted websites and verify HTTPS before entering information.

Trusted Downloads

Only install software from official websites and trusted sources.

Backup Data

Regular backups help recover files after ransomware or data loss.

Cyber Myths vs Facts

❌ Common Myths

  • Antivirus can stop every cyber attack.
  • Hackers only target large companies.
  • Strong passwords alone are enough.
  • Public Wi-Fi is always safe.

✅ Cyber Facts

  • No security tool provides 100% protection.
  • Small businesses are common targets.
  • 2FA greatly improves account security.
  • Public Wi-Fi can expose sensitive data.

Cyber Safety Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do's

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Keep software and apps updated.
  • Verify website URLs before logging in.
  • Backup important files regularly.
  • Use antivirus and security tools.

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't share passwords or OTPs.
  • Don't click suspicious links.
  • Don't reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Don't download files from unknown sources.
  • Don't trust unexpected emails blindly.
  • Don't ignore security updates.

Stay Alert Online

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. Safe online habits, awareness, and smart decisions are the best ways to protect yourself from cyber threats. Stay informed, stay alert, and stay secure.
